summary: |
  On June 17, 1978, the bodies of a man and a woman were discovered near the Missouri-Arkansas border in Pemiscot County. The victims were initially unidentified and referred to as "Pemiscot County John and Jane Doe." Decades later, through forensic genealogy and DNA matching, the victims were identified as Jimmy Hendricks and Kim Mills.

  Despite this identification, the circumstances of their murders remain unknown. The case has seen multiple agencies involved, and investigators continue to seek information that might lead to those responsible for their deaths.
