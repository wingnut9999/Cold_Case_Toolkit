summary: |
  On June 7, 1992, three women — Sherrill Levitt, her daughter Suzanne Streeter, and Suzanne’s friend Stacy McCall — vanished without a trace from Levitt’s home in Springfield, Missouri. Streeter and McCall had just graduated from Kickapoo High School earlier that day and were planning to spend the night at Levitt’s house. When friends arrived the next morning, the women were gone, their cars and purses were still at the residence, and there were no signs of struggle.

  Despite extensive investigation, public appeals, and numerous tips, no substantial evidence has been found explaining what happened to the three. Police have received thousands of leads over three decades, and the case remains one of Missouri’s most perplexing unsolved disappearances, presumed to be a triple homicide.
