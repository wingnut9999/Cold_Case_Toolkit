Fawn Marie Cox Homicide
July 26, 1989
On July 26, 1989 Fawn Marie Cox was raped and murdered in her home at 4640 E. 9th Street KCMO, she was only 16 years old at the time.  2019 is the 30th anniversary of this unsolved homicide.  

 

Please submit any information on this crime 24/7 to Crime Stoppers via our website, FREE P3Tips mobile app or by calling (816) 474-8477.
